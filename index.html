The everyday challenge

Business activity happens in different places. Keeping the records together is the hard part.

Order details, payment notifications and stock counts can live in separate tools. Business owners may need to piece the story together by hand.

⌁

WhatsApp conversations

Customer orders arrive through message threads.

↗

M-Pesa payment messages

Owners must identify and reconcile incoming payments with the right orders.

▦

Stock records

Inventory may be tracked separately in spreadsheets, notebooks, or other records.

◎

Customer balances

Outstanding payments and credit arrangements can be difficult to track consistently.

▤

Daily business reporting

Sales, stock movement, and payment information must be brought together manually.

Illustrative business scenario

A customer orders 12 cartons through WhatsApp. The owner later receives an M-Pesa payment notification. The order, payment, stock movement, and any remaining customer balance must still be reconciled.

The frequency and financial cost of these problems are being tested through customer discovery.

Current market hypothesis

Starting with small wholesalers and distributors.

We believe the operational challenge may be especially relevant to businesses managing repeat orders, regular stock movement, and customer credit. This is our current market hypothesis, and we are testing it through customer interviews.

✓ Regular repeat orders and changing stock

✓ WhatsApp-based customer communication

Planned validation milestones

Validate the problem before scaling the solution.

These are planned milestones, not completed achievements.

DAYS 1–30

Discovery

  • Conduct 30 business-owner interviews
  • Shadow 10 business operations
  • Test whether at least 20 of 30 report weekly stock or payment mismatches
  • Compare wholesale and retail segments
  • Investigate willingness to pay
DAYS 31–60

Prototype

  • Build the focused MVP
  • Prioritise order capture and M-Pesa payment matching
  • Recruit eight design partners
  • Test the core workflow
DAYS 61–90

Pilot

  • Target five businesses using the pilot weekly
  • Seek three paying pilot customers at any price
  • Measure usage and reliability
  • Track customer support time
  • Evaluate willingness to continue paying

Planned milestones only. No customer interviews, prototype recruitment, pilot usage, or paid pilot results are claimed as completed.
